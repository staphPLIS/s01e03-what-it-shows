The three problems

  1. The shared parser reads dates month first (379 reports). All our tenants write the day first. When the day is 12 or less, the parser swaps the day and the month: “5 March is now 3 May”. This is the fix that is queued. The count includes the one-day moves filed here.
  2. The booking-v3 form hint says month first (33 reports). These reports come only through the booking form of tenants on booking-v3. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first. The form then reads the date day first. The parser fix does not change this. The hint and the read must agree.
  3. The date moves by one day (5 reports). No day and month swap occurs: “Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday”. The desk read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and filed them under the parser by tenant, where they are also counted. The pattern is a time zone problem, not a date order problem.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports when their subject lines match. All three problems come in with the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the desk merged them into one ticket. Subject lines tell us that a date is wrong. They do not tell us why.
